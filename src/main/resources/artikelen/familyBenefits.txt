Can I get family benefits for my children?
In Germany and the Netherlands, the government contributes to childcare costs. For example, by paying family allowances or other types of family benefits.

Family benefits from Germany
Because you work in Germany and are covered by social insurance there, you can receive child benefits from Germany. You receive child benefits from the Familienkasse Rheinland-Pfalz – Saarland in Saarbrücken. Want to know more? See the explanation under Child Benefit.

Family benefits from the Netherlands
You do not receive child benefits or child-related budgets. You do receive childcare allowance if you meet the conditions. See the explanation under Childcare Allowance. Are you raising your child with another parent or caregiver? That other parent or caregiver, for example, your partner or ex-partner, may be eligible for child benefits and child-related budgets.
Family benefits from Germany and the Netherlands
If two countries can pay family benefits, European priority rules apply. One country must have priority over the other. The other country may pay supplementary family allowances. Which country pays which amount depends partly on your family circumstances. Want to know more about the priority rules for overlapping family allowances? See the explanation under Family allowances from two countries.
Other family benefits
In addition to family allowances, Germany also offers other family benefits. Are you or your partner expecting a child? Or have you recently become a parent or caregiver? Inquire about maternity benefits (Mutterschaftsgeld), parental leave (Elternzeit), and parental leave benefits (Elterngeld). Want to know more? See the explanation under Other family benefits.
