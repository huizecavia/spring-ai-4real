When do I need to show my ID?
Police and supervisors, such as conductors and labor inspectors, may ask for your identity. Healthcare providers and employers may also check your identity. Therefore, you must have a valid passport or ID card.

What is a valid ID?
You can identify yourself in Germany with a:

German passport, German identity card, or electronic identity card (eID)
Passport or ID card from an EU or EEA country
Passport from another country with a valid residence permit sticker

Where do I apply for a valid ID?
Do you not have a valid ID? Or has your current ID expired? Then apply for an ID card. Where you need to apply depends on your nationality.

Are you a Dutch citizen? Apply at your municipality of residence.
Do you have German nationality? Apply at the German embassy in The Hague.
