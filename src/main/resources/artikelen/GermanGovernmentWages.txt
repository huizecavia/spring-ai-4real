German Government Wages
You work for the German government. You work in Germany and the Netherlands. You pay taxes in Germany on your wages. Your employer withholds the tax from your wages. This is called Lohnsteuer (tax-deductible). The amount of tax withheld depends on your personal situation.

Please note: Are you temporarily receiving social security benefits, for example, due to the birth of your child? This may affect your tax liability. This must be assessed by the Tax and Customs Administration. The Tax and Customs Administration determines which country the payment should be made in. This depends on the situation.

For more information, please contact the Cross-Border Work and Entrepreneurship Team (Team GWO) of the Tax and Customs Administration.
